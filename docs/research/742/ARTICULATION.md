# Articulation contract

The browser exposes five bounded presentation channels plus a three-mode steering selector.

1. Boom lift rotates the three-section boom around the reconstructed rear chassis pivot, between a reconstructed zero-degree ground-clear stow and 69 degrees. Zero degrees places the neutral fork load surface at 0.388 m and the complete rear-plane-to-tip length at 6.9772 m. The maximum-reach presentation uses a separately selected reconstructed 3-degree pose; no official source establishes that angle as the manufacturer maximum-reach datum. The 69-degree endpoint is visible on the published chart. The browser does not reproduce chart capacities.
2. Telescope translates the second section 3.604 m and the chain-driven third section an additional 3.604 m along the boom axis. The 3.604 m telescope-cylinder stroke is evidence-derived; the exact owned section origins and chain transfer geometry remain reconstruction. At full extension the solver retains 1.826 m base/mid and 1.626 m mid/fly visual overlap, and the upper surface of level forks reaches 12.7989 m at 69 degrees against the published 12.8 m maximum lift height.
3. Carriage tilt keeps the standard floating carriage controllable around its boom-head pivot. The asymmetric reconstructed range is 5 degrees down and 12 degrees up. Across the full sampled combination of lift, telescope, tilt, frame level, steering, and modes, the lowest fork blade remains 0.1656 m above the flat-floor proxy. The neutral stow has a 0.328 m blade bottom and 0.388 m load surface.
4. Steering is solved from one translated opposed-rod rack and two invariant steering bars per axle, not from imposed wheel angles. The rear linkage is the longitudinal mirror of the front, so circle mode produces FL/FR/RL/RR angles of 27.477/55.000/-27.477/-55.000 degrees at positive maximum command rather than swapping the rear inner and outer wheels. A 2,001-step four-wheel ICR sweep measures a maximum 0.4342 percent relative center spread, reduced from 10.4624 percent. The evidence-derived service limit is 55 degrees; the rack offset, 0.330959 m steering bars, and resulting linkage curve remain reconstructed rather than factory calibration. Exact nonzero crab parallelism and an Ackermann split cannot both emerge from one continuous translated rack with invariant bars. Crab and front-only presentation commands are conservatively limited to 15 percent rack travel; their actual per-wheel angles and residuals are reported rather than multiplying the UI input by 55 degrees. Front-only mode leaves the rear rack neutral and reaches maximum inner angle 5.181 degrees with dense maximum front-wheel ICR relative spread 4.8694 percent. These are presentation input boundaries, not claimed factory controller limits or calibrations. At maximum circle command the owned geometry produces a 3.7148 m outside wheel-center path and the actual posed tire envelope is 4.3500 m, 18.85 percent above the published 3.66 m figure; these are explicitly not equated because the publication does not resolve the reference locus. Body sweep is reported separately.
5. Frame level rolls the chassis/boom/cab branch around a reconstructed longitudinal axis at 0.82 m visual height up to the published 10-degree limit while the ground-running-gear branch remains fixed. That display axis is not claimed to be an axle centerline or a published JLG coordinate.

Each axle is presented as one fixed-length double-ended steering barrel with two straight opposed rods, an invariant 1.735341 m rod-joint span, and separate 0.330959 m rigid steering bars to the moving steering joints; no duplicate hydraulic actuator is shown. Every other cylinder also keeps a fixed visual barrel while only rod exposure changes between its gland and moving pin. One continuous extend-chain assembly is presented as two parallel fixed-length paths, while the single retract path is separately routed. All three use tangent straight legs and polygonal near-180-degree U-wraps outside their moving sheaves. A 2,001-step continuous travel sweep measures less than 8e-15 m total-length drift, zero normalized tangent-dot error, at least 0.003636 m sheave-surface clearance after chain radius, and a maximum 0.003604 m adjacent endpoint step. The two lift-service loops are reconstructed as three fixed articulated spans totaling 3.210 m. Four boom-service cues are reconstructed as fixed-total 5.000 m guide-and-loop routes between the first and second sections, with an eight-segment 0.140 m-radius curved return rather than a three-segment kink. Dense validation requires at least 5 mm surface clearance from every named rigid boom tube, less than 22.5 degrees between adjacent curved-return chords, and less than 5e-15 m total-length drift. Exact hose lengths, guides, bends, chain lengths, pitch, sheave radii, and anchor coordinates remain reconstructed.

The boom-angle sensor is presented as a fixed rotary body, an invariant 0.10 m crank, and an invariant 0.42 m ball-jointed connecting rod solved by circle intersection to the moving first-section pin. Those dimensions and pin coordinates remain reconstructed. The axle housings are transverse, with a central simplified differential cue and short longitudinal input flange. Named rigid-underbody clearance is swept through all 41 sampled frame-level states, not checked only at neutral. The published 0.432 m ground-clearance value does not identify an exact component locus, so this remains an approximate visual acceptance rather than a differential-clearance claim.

The visual pin-distance deltas reconcile to the cited strokes: lift 1.070000/1.070 m, telescope 3.604000/3.604 m, compensation 0.278000/0.278 m, head tilt 0.387999/0.388 m, and frame sway 0.167996/0.168 m. The rear-axle-stabilization cue uses only 0.105582 m of the published 0.200 m cylinder stroke because the explorer shows frame-level-induced endpoint motion only; it does not simulate free, slow, or locked RAS states.

`machines/742/solver.js` is the single executable pose authority. Browser articulation imports it directly; Blender build/render and the Python validator invoke it through `scripts/solve_742_pose.mjs`. The validator executes 416,745 distinct input states across lift, telescope, tilt, frame level, steering input, and all three steering modes, plus 2,001-state chain, hose, and steering sweeps. It asserts fixed barrels, positive rod exposure, invariant rigid links, fixed-total service-line routes, chain length/tangency/clearance, actual tread-plane reach, named rigid-underbody clearance, exact runtime stow length, maximum-height level forks, the selected 3-degree/full-extension reach pose, and evidence stroke usage. `scripts/run_742_posed_glb_gate.py` launches Blender to import and pose the actual binary through the same bridge. It measures named mesh endpoints, steering-bar lengths, hose totals, frame-level clearance, fork presets, and reconstructed tire/body turning envelopes; both the package gate and receipt replay execute it continuously. This is geometry continuity evidence—not manufacturer dynamics, load, stability, interlock, service, or safety evidence.

The stow command resets lift, telescope, carriage tilt, steering, and frame level. The showcase command animates a deterministic display sequence. Neither command represents operating procedure.
