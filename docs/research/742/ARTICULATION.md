# Articulation contract

The browser exposes five bounded presentation channels plus a three-mode steering selector.

1. Boom lift rotates the three-section boom around the reconstructed rear chassis pivot, between a reconstructed zero-degree ground-clear stow and 69 degrees. Zero degrees places the neutral fork load surface at 0.388 m and the complete rear-plane-to-tip length at 6.9772 m. The distinct published maximum-reach check remains 3 degrees; the 69-degree endpoint is visible on the published chart. The browser does not reproduce chart capacities.
2. Telescope translates the second section 3.604 m and the chain-driven third section an additional 3.604 m along the boom axis. The 3.604 m telescope-cylinder stroke is evidence-derived; the exact owned section origins and chain transfer geometry remain reconstruction. At full extension the solver retains 1.826 m base/mid and 1.626 m mid/fly visual overlap, and the upper surface of level forks reaches 12.7989 m at 69 degrees against the published 12.8 m maximum lift height.
3. Carriage tilt keeps the standard floating carriage controllable around its boom-head pivot. The asymmetric reconstructed range is 5 degrees down and 12 degrees up. Across the full sampled combination of lift, telescope, tilt, frame level, steering, and modes, the lowest fork blade remains 0.1656 m above the flat-floor proxy. The neutral stow has a 0.328 m blade bottom and 0.388 m load surface.
4. Steering uses nonlinear wheelbase/track Ackermann geometry rather than a constant inner/outer multiplier. The evidence-derived service limit is 55 degrees at the inner wheel. With the 3.42 m published wheelbase and 2.1005 m temporary-reference wheel-center track, symmetric circle steering produces a 27.4-degree outer wheel; front-only steering produces 37.3 degrees. Circle mode assigns opposite front/rear axle angles about one instantaneous center, crab uses four parallel wheels, and front mode leaves the rear wheels neutral. The owned geometry produces a 3.7148 m outside wheel-center path. The published 3.66 m outside turning radius is reported independently because its reference locus and tire swept-envelope convention are unresolved; the two numbers are not treated as equivalent or as manufacturer steering calibration.
5. Frame level rolls the chassis/boom/cab branch around a reconstructed longitudinal axis at 0.82 m visual height up to the published 10-degree limit while the ground-running-gear branch remains fixed. That display axis is not claimed to be an axle centerline or a published JLG coordinate.

Each axle is presented as one fixed-length double-ended steering barrel with two straight opposed rods and separate left/right steering bars to the moving steering joints; no duplicate hydraulic actuator is shown. Every other cylinder also keeps a fixed visual barrel while only rod exposure changes between its gland and moving pin. The two extend-chain cues and three-part retract-chain route, plus boom hose/tube cues, run inside or below the boom. The retract route has separated fixed, sheave-wrap, and moving legs; a 2,001-step continuous travel sweep retains at least 0.18 m segment length and 0.18 m leg separation with a maximum 0.003604 m endpoint step. Exact anchors and flexible routing remain reconstructed.

The visual pin-distance deltas reconcile to the cited strokes: lift 1.070000/1.070 m, telescope 3.604000/3.604 m, compensation 0.278000/0.278 m, head tilt 0.387999/0.388 m, and frame sway 0.167996/0.168 m. The rear-axle-stabilization cue uses only 0.105582 m of the published 0.200 m cylinder stroke because the explorer shows frame-level-induced endpoint motion only; it does not simulate free, slow, or locked RAS states.

`machines/742/solver.js` is the single executable pose authority. Browser articulation imports it directly; Blender build/render and the Python validator invoke it through `scripts/solve_742_pose.mjs`. The validator actually executes 416,745 distinct input states across lift, telescope, tilt, frame level, steering input, and all three steering modes, plus the 2,001-state chain sweep. It asserts fixed barrel length, positive rod exposure, posed-GLB hierarchy, section overlap, fork clearance, exact runtime stow length, the maximum-height level-fork pose, the separate 3-degree/full-extension maximum-reach pose, evidence stroke usage, and Ackermann center agreement. This is geometry continuity evidence—not manufacturer dynamics, load, stability, interlock, service, or safety evidence.

The stow command resets lift, telescope, carriage tilt, steering, and frame level. The showcase command animates a deterministic display sequence. Neither command represents operating procedure.
