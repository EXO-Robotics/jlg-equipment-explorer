# Detailed reconstruction checklist

- Four rough-terrain tire assemblies with independent tread blocks, wheel faces, hubs, and lug hardware
- Front and rear steer pivots, axle housings, one fixed-barrel double-ended steering cylinder plus separate left/right steering bars per axle, and three steering presentations with mode-specific nonlinear Ackermann geometry
- Chassis rails, counterweight, belly pans, tow points, steps, fenders, lights, grille, and layered engine hood
- Open ROPS/FOPS cab with glazing, seat, steering wheel, dashboard, joystick, pedals, mirrors, roof guard, and handrails
- Three nested boom sections with wear bands and pins; fixed-length barrels with variable rod exposure for the lift, telescope, compensation-master, head-tilt, frame-level, and rear-stabilization cues; two internal/underside extend chains; a separated three-leg retract-chain route around its moving sheave; flexible/rigid line cues; and a frame-mounted rotary boom-angle sensor with moving link
- Quick coupler, floating 50 in carriage, fork pin/collars, backrest, endpoint-rigged head-tilt slave cylinder/link, and two exact 48 in (1.2192 m) pallet forks modeled as 60 mm vertical thickness by 102 mm lateral width
- Frame-level hierarchy separated from ground-running gear, with endpoint-rigged frame-sway and rear-axle-stabilization cylinder cues
- Six transparent semantic interaction volumes
- Independently authored material identities for orange bodywork, cream boom, black steel, rubber, glass, zinc hardware, hoses, interior, lamps, and generic labels

Acceptance is based on named mechanical contracts and dimensions rather than mesh/node-count floors. The browser, Blender builder, renderer, and dense validator consume one executable solver; the hierarchy/GLB gate separately verifies the exported asset. The owned asset receipt, 416,745-state geometry sampling, 2,001-state retract-chain sweep, source comparison renders, browser inspection, accessibility checks, and performance diagnostics remain separate gates. None establishes manufacturer dynamics, load, stability, interlock, service, or safety correctness.
