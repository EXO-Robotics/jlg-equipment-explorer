The owned GLB measures 2.4605 m across its base running gear, within 1 mm of the rounded published 2.46 m width. The left mirror extends the complete visual envelope to 2.5803 m (`-1.3500 m` to `+1.2303 m` laterally), so the mirror-inclusive render is reported separately and is never labeled as the published base-machine width. The current posed-GLB gate also measures 5.7580 m length less forks against 5.76 m, 2.4199 m stowed height against 2.43 m, and a lowest ground-running point 0.00009 m above the floor plane. Small shapes, non-fork sheet thicknesses, tread pattern, actuator anchors, section overlaps, and cab-member dimensions remain independent visual reconstruction.

The selected fork blades are modeled at the exact 48 in imperial selection length, 1.2192 m, with a 0.060 m vertical thickness and 0.102 m lateral width. The specification's metric label rounds the same selection to 1200 mm. The upper load surface in the 69-degree/full-telescope level pose reaches 12.7989 m. A separate 3-degree/full-extension pose places the 24 in (0.6096 m) load center 8.8700 m forward of the front tire plane against the published 8.86 m maximum reach. These are visual envelope reconciliations, not a load chart or operating-envelope implementation.

The official BIM reach pose was triangulated temporarily and measured only as a coarse orientation/envelope check. Its geometry is not present in the `.blend` or `.glb`.
