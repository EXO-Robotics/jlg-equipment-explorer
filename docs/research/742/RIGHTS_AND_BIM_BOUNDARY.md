# Rights and BIM boundary

JLG publications, gallery images, BIM files, trademarks, and product designs remain the property of their respective owners. They were downloaded into temporary evidence storage for research, hashing, inspection, and comparison only.

No manufacturer PDF, raster image, IFC mesh, decal artwork, UV texture, or extracted vertex buffer is committed or deployed. The shipped 742 geometry is authored from Blender primitives and independent curves. Product markings are independently typeset nominative labels, not copied artwork. The repository validator scans the tracked tree for the frozen source filenames and a broad source-binary extension set; the Pages assembly repeats the leakage check on its 742 research payload.

The official IFC package is especially restricted to silhouette, component-location, and coarse envelope checking. The source builder contains no IFC import operation. The builder, Blend file, GLB, evidence ledger, public research notes, runtime, and validators are independently hashed into the candidate receipt. Those hashes preserve the declared provenance chain but do not, by themselves, prove authorship or engineering accuracy.

The 742 candidate receipt is not shipped by Pages and never labels itself deployed. Authorized deployment creates a separate workflow artifact only after an external HTTP probe retrieves the canonical public route and GLB and matches the GLB to the candidate hash. Visual, browser, accessibility, performance, and regression judgments remain pending unless a review manifest binds each result to repository-contained artifacts, an exact reviewed commit, browser, and operating-system environment.
