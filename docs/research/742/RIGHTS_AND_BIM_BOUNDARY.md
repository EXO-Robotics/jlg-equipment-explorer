# Rights and BIM boundary

JLG publications, gallery images, BIM files, trademarks, and product designs remain the property of their respective owners. They were downloaded into temporary evidence storage for research, hashing, inspection, and comparison only.

No manufacturer PDF, raster image, IFC mesh, decal artwork, UV texture, or extracted vertex buffer is committed or deployed. The shipped 742 geometry is authored from Blender primitives and independent curves. Product markings are independently typeset nominative labels, not copied artwork. The only tracked 742 review rasters are six owned Blender renders admitted by exact repository path, SHA-256, byte count, PNG dimensions, and provenance in `docs/review/742/OWNED_RENDER_ALLOWLIST.json`. The repository validator rejects every other source-like binary path and scans all tracked files against every admitted manufacturer hash and size. Pages repeats the manufacturer filename/hash scan and does not package the review renders.

The official IFC package is especially restricted to silhouette, component-location, and coarse envelope checking. The source builder contains no IFC import operation. The builder, Blend file, GLB, evidence ledger, public research notes, runtime, and validators are independently hashed into the candidate receipt. Those hashes preserve the declared provenance chain but do not, by themselves, prove authorship or engineering accuracy.

The 742 candidate receipt is not shipped by Pages and never labels itself deployed. Authorized deployment creates a separate workflow artifact only after external HTTP probes match the public build manifest and the exact route, GLB, CSS, runtime, machine modules, configuration, mechanism record, and public research payload by hash and byte count. Visual, browser, accessibility-semantics-and-keyboard, performance, and regression judgments remain pending unless a review manifest binds each distinct gate artifact to the exact candidate tree, reviewed commit, browser, and operating system. No gate claims physical assistive-technology, physical-device, load, stability, service, safety, or manufacturer-equivalence proof.
