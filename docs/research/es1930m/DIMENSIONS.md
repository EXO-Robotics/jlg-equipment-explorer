These dimensions apply to the cylinder bodies, not to their installation coordinates. Anchor positions remain reconstructed.

## Capacity and operating envelope

- Total platform capacity: 500 lb / 227 kg.
- Extension-deck capacity: 254 lb / 115 kg, included in total platform capacity.
- Occupants: two indoors and one outdoors for the frozen standard machine.
- Rated lift time: 20-25 seconds in R04/R05; R02 publishes 25-26 seconds with rated load. Timing is product behavior, not the interactive viewer's animation duration.
- Rated lower time: 20-26 seconds in R04/R05.

## Reconciled conflicts

The May 2025 launch release states a 57.8 in machine length. Current PVC 2404 operation/service manuals and the current product-linked specification state 58.2 in. The frozen PVC 2404 target therefore uses 58.2 in and records 57.8 in as superseded/non-applicable to this exact freeze.

Minor 78.0/78.1, 29.9/30.0 and 21.6/21.7 in differences are publication rounding, not separate configurations. Runtime uses the metric values published with the current standard specification.

## Prohibited derivations

Overall height does not reveal arm length, pivot spacing or cylinder anchors. Those values must be solved from an authored, visually reconstructed pivot graph and remain marked `reconstructed`; they may not be presented as JLG dimensions.
