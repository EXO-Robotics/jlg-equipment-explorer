# ES1930M source reconciliation

## PVC 2404 is primary

The parts, service and operation set 3122602400/2300/2200 explicitly identifies ES1930M PVC 2404. It controls current configuration, component identity, mechanism topology, dimensions and visible system relationships for this project.

## PVC 1001 standalone schematics

JLG Online Express currently labels standalone hydraulic 3122602600 and electrical 3122602500 as PVC 1001. They are official JLG publications but do not inherit PVC 2404 authority.

### Hydraulic comparison

The standalone sheet publishes the same ES1930M headline hydraulic values found in PVC 2404 service p. 21 and Figure 89: 60 mm lift inner bore, 45 mm lift rod, 685.5 mm lift stroke, 60 mm steer bore, 35 mm steer rod, 80 mm steer travel per direction, 43 bar steering relief and 206 bar main relief. This supports system continuity.

PVC 2404 service evidence remains the cited source for implementation. The standalone sheet may not provide geometry, anchor placement or option applicability.

### Electrical comparison

The standalone seven-sheet drawing lists the same major harness identifiers embedded in PVC 2404 service Figure 90, including chassis LSS `1001323278`, lift-down LSS `1001294314`, platform/AC `1001322990`, platform-control `1001241320`, drive-control `1001243255`, standard battery cable kits `1001243231/3230`, and telematics gateway `1001282845`.

This supports shared system taxonomy. PVC 2404 parts/service pages control the frozen branch and visible routing. Optional lithium, inverter, overhead-protection and other branches remain excluded unless separately admitted.

## Configuration branches

The manuals contain multiple valid configurations. The frozen standard machine excludes:

- integrated mid-rail deck;
- HVAC and ceiling-tile platform configurations;
- AGM and lithium battery branches;
- fold-down/telescopic rail branches;
- inverter, perimeter-lighting, hostile-environment and overhead-protection options.

The existence of an assembly in a current parts manual does not make it standard equipment.

## Length conflict

The 2025 launch release's 57.8 in overall length conflicts with the 58.2 in value repeated by current PVC 2404 operation/service manuals and current specification 3131313. The freeze uses 58.2 in and retains the older figure only as historical context.

## Fail-closed rules

1. A cross-PVC claim cannot carry `verified` geometry authority.
2. A parts illustration can establish identity and topology but not unprinted dimensions.
3. Service procedures can establish attachment and motion relationships but not fabrication coordinates.
4. Product photos can establish silhouette and visible configuration, not hidden components.
5. Optional assemblies remain absent unless the configuration file explicitly selects them.
